## New Environmental Profiles

### THOS-BIO
Biological/ecological telemetry: animal movement, migration shifts, pollination, acoustic behavior, vegetation stress, canopy health, biodiversity.

### THOS-ENV
Environmental state: soil chemistry, groundwater, contamination, mineral inference, erosion, aquifer stress, ecosystem coherence.

### THOS-ATMOS
Atmospheric telemetry: air quality, wind, temperature, humidity, smoke/pollution plumes, storm development, precipitation, solar radiation.

### THOS-CAD
Geometry, design, model, and build-state telemetry.

### THOS-MAP
Geospatial features and map-state transitions.
